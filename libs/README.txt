Place the following JAR files in this directory:

1. create-6.0.10.jar  - The Create mod compiled JAR
2. netmusic.jar       - The NetMusic mod compiled JAR

You can find these JARs in the build/libs/ directories of the respective mod projects,
or download them from CurseForge/Modrinth.

After placing the JARs, update build.gradle to reference the correct filenames.
